My pick: 2 for Argon, 3 for Argus

The full shell (2) is the most distinctive and tells the pitch on its own: argon is inert because its outer shell is full, so it never reacts alone, just as the AI never decides alone. It stays readable down to 16 px. The Argus eye (3) then marks the AI: same weight, its own shape, and the violet iris is the colour argon glows when it is energised. That is the Rovo pattern: the assistant has its own mark, clearly related to the product's.

The element tile (1) is the safest choice and closest to today's "Ar" square, but periodic-table tiles are common, so it is the least memorable.

SVG files: docs/design/logos/. Colours come from CSS variables (--logo-ink, --logo-on, --logo-accent, --logo-glow), so the same file works in light and dark mode. For a final version, turn the "18" in option 1 into outlines so it does not depend on the font.
