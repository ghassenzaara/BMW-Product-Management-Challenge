# Equipment Baseline: What Each Car Offers Today (German Price Lists, July 2026)

English summary of the three option-list PDFs in [`raw/`](raw/). Use it to check whether a customer want is **already available** (standard or optional), only **missing in some variants**, or **not offered at all** (a true gap). Full searchable text (German, layout preserved) is in [`processed/option-lists/`](processed/option-lists/).

**Caveats**
- These are **German market** lists (prices in EUR including 19% VAT). Equipment and prices differ in the US and China.
- Prices and option codes (SA-Nr.) were matched from layout text, where the price column is not always aligned with its item. Prices marked `~` are best-effort. Check the PDF before quoting them.
- The G70 list describes the **updated 7 Series** (BMW Panoramic Vision, Panoramic iDrive, Operating System X). That is newer than the cars most of the feedback comes from.

## Shared "Baukasten" (toolbox) components across series
Many items repeat across the three lists at different levels, which is the modular toolbox Patrick described `[PM2]`:

| Component | 1 Series | 5 Series | 7 Series |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating System | OS 9 | OS 8.5 | OS X + Panoramic iDrive |
| Display concept | Curved Display 10.25 + 10.7" | Curved Display 12.3 + 14.9" | Panoramic Vision + 3D HUD + Passenger Screen |
| Head-up display | Option (Innovation pkg) | Option (Innovation pkg) | Standard (3D) |
| Driving Assistant | Standard / Plus / Professional | Standard / Plus / Professional + Motorway | Plus standard, Motorway & City option |
| Parking Assistant | Standard / Plus / Professional | Standard / Plus / Professional | Plus standard |
| Seat ventilation / massage | – | Front ventilation (Comfort pkg) | Front + rear (packages) |
| Heated steering wheel | Option €250 | Option | Standard |
| Wireless charging | Premium pkg | Standard | Standard |
| Climate | 2-zone | 4-zone | 4-zone + parked climatisation |

A requirement found for one series (e.g. "physical shortcut buttons", "permanent MSA off") is therefore often a **cross-series toolbox requirement**. Specify it once and set the level per series.
