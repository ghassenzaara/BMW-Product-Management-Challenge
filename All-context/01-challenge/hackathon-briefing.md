# BMW Group – AI for Product Decision Making

## The challenge

What should the next generation of a BMW vehicle offer its customers? Build an AI-powered application that helps product managers derive, justify, and prioritize customer requirements for a successor vehicle. Ground your proposals in customer feedback, BMW product and sales data, and relevant web research—while keeping the product manager firmly in control of every decision.

## Why this matters

Defining a new vehicle generation means understanding what customers value today while anticipating what they will value when its successor reaches the market. Product Managers are turning large volumes of customer feedback, market signals, competitor information, and product data into well-founded product requirements. Today, this work is largely manual, time-consuming, and difficult to trace consistently. AI could help product managers identify relevant insights, formulate requirements, and justify prioritization decisions more efficiently, while keeping evidence, assumptions, and uncertainties transparent and under human control.

## Your mission

Develop an application that helps product managers define, explain, and prioritize customer-facing requirements for the successor of an existing BMW vehicle line.

- Use the BMW 1 Series, 5 Series, 7 Series, or a combination of them, and focus on Europe, China, the United States, or any subset that best demonstrates your approach. Think three to five years ahead and identify the customer needs and expectations that should shape the next generation of the vehicle.
- Make clear where your conclusions are based on available evidence and where they rely on forward-looking assumptions.
- For each vehicle, BMW will provide customer feedback, equipment data, sales volumes, and a customer study. You may enrich these inputs with external sources such as competitor information, reviews, and market or technology trends. Use this information to identify relevant customer needs, opportunities, or pain points and translate them into well-founded product requirements.
- BMW is interested in how your solution turns diverse information into actionable product-management insights and makes its reasoning transparent, not in a specific workflow or technical architecture.

## What to demonstrate

- **A concept and working prototype** that shows how AI supports the identification, formulation, and prioritization of customer-facing product requirements using the provided data and, where relevant, external sources.
- **A representative set of requirements supported by clear evidence and reasoning.** Demonstrate how customer feedback, product data, market signals, or competitor insights translate into requirements and explain the factors driving their prioritization.
- **A transparent decision-support experience** that allows product managers to review, challenge, modify, approve, or reject AI-generated proposals. Show how evidence, assumptions, uncertainties, and key decisions remain visible and traceable throughout the process.

## Make it yours!

- Focus on the area where your team can create the most value, whether that is uncovering customer insights, combining BMW and external data, formulating compelling requirements, improving prioritization, or designing an effective human-in-the-loop experience.
- You do not need to develop every capability to the same level of maturity. Instead, demonstrate how your chosen approach helps product managers make better-informed decisions.
- Consider how the concept could scale across vehicle lines, markets, or future product decisions.
- BMW values: trustworthy sources, transparent reasoning, thoughtful prioritization, and a clear understanding of uncertainty and conflicting evidence.
- Focus on **customer value** rather than engineering specifications, regulatory requirements, or detailed business-case calculations.
